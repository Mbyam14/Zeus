Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2, 2026

Zeus Pantry ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use the Zeus Pantry mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following information when you use Zeus Pantry:

  • Account information: Email address, username, and password (stored as a secure hash)
  • Profile information: Dietary preferences, food allergies, calorie goals, and cuisine preferences you provide
  • App usage data: Meal plans you create, recipes you save or like, pantry items, and grocery lists
  • AI conversation history: Messages you send to the Zeus AI assistant
  • Analytics data: App interactions used to improve the service (e.g. features used, screens visited)

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and personalize the Zeus Pantry service
  • To generate AI-powered meal plans and recipe recommendations tailored to your preferences
  • To maintain your pantry, grocery lists, and meal history
  • To improve app features and fix issues
  • To communicate with you about your account if necessary

3. Third-Party Services

Zeus Pantry uses the following third-party services to operate:

  • Supabase — Secure database and authentication provider. Your data is stored on Supabase servers.
  • Anthropic (Claude AI) — Powers the Zeus AI assistant. Messages sent to the AI are processed by Anthropic's servers. See Anthropic's Privacy Policy.
  • Railway — Cloud hosting provider for our backend servers.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We do not share your data with advertisers.

4. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us at the email below. Account deletion removes your meal plans, pantry, grocery lists, and conversation history.

5. Children's Privacy

Zeus Pantry is intended for users aged 12 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 12. If you believe a child under 12 has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

6. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including encrypted data transmission (HTTPS), secure password hashing, and access controls. However, no method of internet transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Rights (California Residents)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we collect about you
  • Request deletion of your personal information
  • Opt out of the sale of personal information (we do not sell your data)

To exercise these rights, contact us at ZeusAI@gmail.com.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

If you have any questions about this privacy policy or your data, please contact us at:
